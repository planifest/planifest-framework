## Dominant pattern: cross-file duplication, not per-file bloat

All three audits independently converged on the same structural finding: the largest recoverable volume is content repeated near-verbatim across multiple files, not padding within any single file. Notable examples:
- Skills: "Commit Cadence (Hard Limit 7)" byte-identical in 6 files (and says so — "this skill adds no local variation"); telemetry emission-gate paragraph verbatim in 7 files; "one question at a time" expanded identically in 9 places.
- Standards: `code-quality-standards.md` §10 duplicates `testing-standards.md` almost point for point — and both are bundled together by codegen-agent and validate-agent, so it loads twice in the same context.
- Templates: provenance/footer boilerplate (`**Tool:**`/`**Model:**` lines, `*Generated by X...*` footers) repeated across 12-15 templates; grep-verified nothing outside the templates themselves reads those fields.

## Explicitly protected (confirmed load-bearing, not touched by any recommendation)

Every Hard Limit, STOP gate, and hook-enforced string was checked against the actual hook/test source, not assumed:
- `gate-write.mjs` / `check-design.mjs` regex-parse `## Scope` in `design.template.md`
- `ratchet-check.mjs` matches `## In Scope`, `## Acceptance Criteria`, and `- [ ]` syntax verbatim
- `commit-msg` hook cites `commit-standards.md` by section number (§1/§3/§4)
- `database-standards.md` Hard Limits 3/4 (destructive migration block)
- `monorepo-standards.md` `src/{component}/` tree structure gate-write.mjs matches against
- `loop-state.template.md` `status: active` (arms ratchet-check)
- test-0000018's `build-log.template.md` per-phase block assertion (`grep -c '| Telemetry |' >= 2`) — this is why `build-log.template.md` lands at only 4% despite looking like an obvious duplicate at first glance
- "Credentials are never in your context" — duplicated in 7 files, but it's a Hard Limit in every one and those are independent fresh-context subagents; explicitly marked **do not remove** despite the duplication pattern
